# Research agenda

## Question
**How much does provenance-preserving, access-filtered retrieval reduce unsupported downstream use of private business documents?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Sensitivity filtering prevents obvious cross-scope leakage in controlled corpora.

### H2
A minimum-evidence gate reduces unsupported downstream action when retrieval coverage is poor.

### H3
Traceable source packets improve reviewer ability to identify retrieval mistakes.

## Experiments
1. Create mixed public/private corpora and measure unauthorized retrieval under different allowed-sensitivity sets.
2. Vary distractor density and query ambiguity; compare gated and ungated downstream decisions.
3. Add conflicting chunks and measure whether reviewers catch contradictions using source-preserving packets.

## Metrics
- retrieval precision/recall
- sensitivity leakage rate
- insufficient-evidence detection
- source traceability
- reviewer contradiction-detection rate
